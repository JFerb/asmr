# Week 9 — Recap

## Overview

The final session closes the semester arc. There are no new exercises and nothing to submit.

Two students (or groups) present their MiniLab solutions to the group — one for MiniLab 1 (reactive maze navigation) and one for MiniLab 2 (localisation + arm trajectory). The rest of the session maps the full semester onto the PRA loop and opens up to questions.

## Session Format

- **Student presentations:** live demo of MiniLab 1 and MiniLab 2 solutions
- **PRA journey map:** every week placed on a timeline, labelled with which phase(s) it covered
- **Open Q&A**
- **Future directions:** SLAM, Nav2, MoveIt, reinforcement learning — what the full professional pipeline looks like beyond this course
